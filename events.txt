This file documents event triggers that effectively function as keys and that,
if pre-triggered or added to the pool, could make the game less linear. It also
documents door latches that provide significant shortcuts once opened.

## Everywhere - dummies

These normally spawn only after certain events and are thus available only on
backtracking. Pre-spawning them would make it easier for the player to tune
weapons, if needed.

## Catacombs - The Lamenting Mother

Initially, the doors connecting Shrine to the Martyrs to Hall of Dying Hope, and
Lamenting Mother to The Last Stab of Hope, are rusted shut. They do not open
until you defeat the ghost in Lamenting Mother and exit west to Martyrs, at
which point an earthquake opens both doors, but also replaces the original
version of Mother with one that is split into east/west halves.

If these doors started out open, it would remove the need to visit Mother to
gain access to northwest Catacombs, and also make Mother usable as a shortcut
to get to the east half of Catacombs slightly early.

## Sanctum - Passage of the Refugees

A cloudstone here grants access to the north shore of Advent Ground and thus to
City Centre West. In normal play it is not active until the Golem is defeated in
Hall of Sacrilege. If active most of Sanctum becomes optional.

## Town Centre West - Tircolas Flow

A cloudstone here connects the north and south halves of the region. It is
inactive on first visit and activates only after reaching the south half by
other means. I'm not sure if the exact trigger is entering Rue Bouquet, entering
Tircolas Flow from the south, killing Ogre, or something else entirely, but if
it started out active, the entirety of City Walls West and Abandoned Mines B1
become optional.

## Town Centre West - Villeport Way

A latched door opens into In Wait of the Foe, letting you skip straight to City
Walls South (and thus to the Keep, Town Centre South, and points beyond) without
going through Undercity West and Snowfly Forest.

## Snowfly Forest - The Yellow Wood

The south exit leading to Forest River is inaccessible until the Earth Dragon in
Return to the Land. Pretriggering this only lets us skip the Earth Dragon and
a few rooms around it, though.

## Town Centre South - Valdiman Gates

Latch to The Boy's Training Room. Lets you go straight from City Walls South to
Town Centre South without visiting The Keep, which doesn't skip very many rooms
but *does* let you skip the first Rosencrantz fight entirely.

## Undercity West - Crumbling Market

Latch to Hall of Poverty. This connects the west quarter (with access to Town
Centre West and Snowfly Forest) to the east (with access to Town Centre South,
northeast Abandoned Mines B2, and Town Centre East), allowing you to completely
bypass Snowfly Forest, Walls South and East, The Keep, and Town Centre South,
going directly from TCW to TCE by cutting through UCW.

## Town Centre East - The House Gilgitte

Latch to Rue Crimnade. This connects Great Cathedral and Temple of Kiltia to
the rest of TCE, allowing access to the Cathdral without going through
Undercity East, Limestone Quarry, and Temple of Kiltia.
