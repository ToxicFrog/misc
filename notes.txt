# Overall game flow

This is the intended route to beat the game, based on how keys and stuff
are positioned, at a fairly high level of abstraction.

A - means the area needs to be revisited later. A + means the area is now fully explored except for NG+ areas. A * means it is fully explored.

* Wine Cellar
* Catacombs
* Sanctum
- Town Centre West (north half)
* City Walls West
+ Abandoned Mines B1
* Town Centre West (south half)
- Undercity West W
+ Snowfly Forest
+ City Walls South
+ The Keep
- Iron Maiden B1 (to get sigil)
+ Town Centre South
+ City Walls East
- Undercity West S
- Abandoned Mines B2 (brief detour)
- Undercity West E
Optional: Abandoned Mines B2 time trial via TCS
Optional: remainder of Iron Maiden B1
- City Walls North
- Town Centre East
- Undercity East (iron key)
! Teleport and iron key acquired, so a bunch of backtracking to earlier areas
  to get keys and sigils and stuff
- Limestone Quarry
- Temple of Kiltia (silver key)
- The Cathedral

On second play, more or less the same until you have the iron and silver keys. Once you have those:

- Escapeway (gold key) via UCW
- Godhands workshop via UCW
- Snowfly Forest East (platinum key) via CWE
- Forgotten Pathway (steel key) via TK
- Iron Maiden B2/B3
- boss time trials
